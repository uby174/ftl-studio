=== FTL-1 KEY (how to read the program below) ===
You are reading FTL-1, the Funnel-Tree Language: a compressed program
describing one video. Decode it with these rules:
1. SEED:: is the concept in plain words.
2. LAW:: sets authority between element types:
   ID(identity) > AR(archetype) > MO(mood) > WO(world) > DE(scene).
   If two elements ever disagree, obey the higher one and adapt the lower.
3. NODE blocks each describe one element. Their six keys mean:
   form=shape/anatomy  mat=surface/texture  mot=how it moves
   lit=how light behaves on it  snd=the sound it makes  phy=physics acting on it.
4. RESOLVE:: lines (A (x) B -> C) are pre-decided conflicts: always apply C.
5. FREEZE blocks are canon. $BIBLE = the character exactly as written,
   $WORLD = the location exactly as written. Expand them in full wherever
   referenced. Never reinterpret, restyle, or abbreviate them.
6. SHOT blocks are renderable clips:
   cam()=camera+lens  act{}=action  lit()=lighting  aud{}=all audio
   anc()=continuity element carried from the previous shot  sty{}=film style.
7. Render ONLY the shot named on the RENDER:: line, as one continuous
   photorealistic cinematic clip. Everything not in that shot is context.
=== END KEY ===

=== FTL-1Q ADDENDUM (cinema-grade quality laws) ===
13. LOOK { } is a frozen craft block -- the film's photographic DNA. Its keys
    (dof, grain, palette, light-source, camera-speed) apply to EVERY shot,
    on top of that shot's sty{}.
14. QUALITY LAWS -- obey in every shot:
    - ONE small, precise physical action per shot; hands, faces, and props
      do the acting. Small exact motion renders masterfully; big motion breaks.
    - Every lit() names the physical light source inside the scene (window,
      doorway, lamp, fire, suns). Never unmotivated light.
    - Shallow depth of field by default: one sharp subject plane, creamy
      falloff. Deep focus only when a shot explicitly asks.
    - The camera moves slowly or not at all; the subject supplies the motion.
    - Every surface carries age: wear, dust, patina, scratches, skin texture.
15. Every sequence contains at least one INSERT shot -- an extreme close-up
    of hands, a prop, or a material detail on a macro lens. It is the film's
    most tactile moment.
=== END ADDENDUM ===

SEED:: "a howdy cowboy Yoda from Star Wars"
LAW::  ID > AR > MO > WO > DE

NODE yoda [ID]
  form: 66cm elderly alien; oversized head; large pointed drooping ears;
        wispy white hair; deep wrinkles; heavy-lidded knowing eyes;
        three-fingered hands; three-toed feet; cane in left hand
  mat:  matte green wrinkled skin, subtle subsurface glow; papery around eyes
  mot:  slow, deliberate, weight of 900 years; uncanny speed only via the Force
  lit:  ears translucent when backlit -- always rim-light them
  snd:  gravelly breathy voice, inverted syntax, soft "hmm"
  phy:  light body -- dust barely stirs at his steps; robe hems drag

NODE cowboy [AR]
  form: duster coat, wide-brim hat, gun-belt slung low -- all child-scale
  mat:  weathered brown leather; sun-bleached tan felt; trail dust in creases
  mot:  saloon-door push, hat tip, unhurried amble
  snd:  belt jingle, leather creak, boardwalk knock
  phy:  duster sways with steps; brim flexes in wind; dust kicks at heels

NODE howdy [MO]
  mot:  two clawed fingers rise to the brim, slow tip, faint smile
  snd:  drawled "Howdy, hmm... partners, you are." -- the room falls quiet
  lit:  warm welcoming key on the face at the moment of greeting

NODE starwars [WO]
  form: used-future frontier: moisture vaporators, droid sweeping a boardwalk
  lit:  twin suns = double shadows; warm tungsten interiors vs hard white exteriors
  snd:  wind, vaporator hum; a low mystical hum when the Force is used
  phy:  fine dust everywhere; objects float only when the Force acts

NODE scene [DE]
  form: saloon on a frontier main street; doorway = hard light blade into dark room
  mot:  arc across shots: enter -> greet -> force-draw a mug

RESOLVE:: human cowboy scale (x) yoda.form -> tailor wardrobe child-scale, notch hat brim for the ears
RESOLVE:: leather boots (x) three-toed feet -> open-toed leather boot-wraps
RESOLVE:: 1870s Earth west (x) starwars -> Tatooine-like frontier town, twin suns
RESOLVE:: quick friendly wave (x) yoda.mot -> slow two-finger brim tip

FREEZE $BIBLE { Yoda, the small elderly green-skinned alien Jedi Master, 66 cm tall,
  with large pointed drooping ears, wispy white hair, deep wrinkles, and heavy-lidded
  knowing eyes, dressed as a frontier cowboy: a child-scale weathered brown leather
  duster over cream Jedi under-robes, a sun-bleached tan cowboy hat with the brim
  notched around his ears, a hand-tooled gun-belt holstering his lightsaber like a
  six-shooter, and open-toed leather boot-wraps over three-toed feet. He moves slowly
  and deliberately, with the weight of 900 years, cane in left hand. }

FREEZE $WORLD { A sun-scorched frontier main street on a Tatooine-like desert world:
  sand-drifted wooden boardwalks, a saloon with swinging doors, moisture vaporators
  turning like windmills, twin suns hanging low, heat-haze shimmering, fine dust
  rolling in the wind. Lived-in used-future western. }

LOOK {
  dof: shallow, f1.8-2.8, one sharp subject plane, creamy falloff
  grain: fine 35mm, gentle halation on bright highlights
  palette: sun-bleached amber, dust-tan, worn leather brown; deep teal shadows
  light-source: every shot lit by a nameable source -- twin suns, doorway blade, oil lamps
  camera-speed: slow or locked; the world moves, the camera barely does
}

SHOT 1 "Entrance" [8s]
  cam(slow dolly-in, 35mm anamorphic, low angle)
  act{ $BIBLE pushes through the saloon's swinging doors, pauses silhouetted
       in the doorway, then steps into the light. $WORLD }
  lit(golden-hour twin-sun backlight, long double shadows, volumetric dust)
  aud{ door creak, belt jingle, wind; the saloon piano stops mid-note }
  sty{ gritty spaghetti-western grain, Kodak 2383 warmth }

SHOT 2 "Howdy" [6s]  anc(doorway light <- SHOT 1)
  cam(medium close-up, 50mm, eye-level with the tiny figure)
  act{ $BIBLE looks up from under the brim, tips it slowly with two clawed
       fingers and drawls: "Howdy, hmm... partners, you are." Faint smile.
       $WORLD :saloon-interior }
  lit(key light from the doorway behind, shallow depth of field)
  aud{ his gravelly line, hat leather creak, murmur dying out }
  sty{ same stock }

SHOT 3 "Force Draw" [7s]  anc(gun-belt <- SHOT 2)
  cam(wide, 24mm, dust at boot level, slow lateral track right)
  act{ a mug slides down the bar; without looking $BIBLE raises his right
       hand -- the mug floats gently into his grip; he sips.
       $WORLD :saloon-interior }
  lit(warm tungsten interior vs hard white doorway light)
  aud{ low mystical hum, wooden slide of the mug, one piano note resumes }
  sty{ same stock }

SHOT 4 "Insert -- The Brim" [5s]  anc(hat <- SHOT 2)
  cam(macro insert, 100mm, extreme close-up, razor-thin focus, locked off)
  act{ two wrinkled green three-knuckled fingers, every crease and old scar
       sharp, slide slowly along the sun-bleached felt brim of the notched
       cowboy hat -- fine dust lifts off the fibers into the light as the
       brim dips a few millimeters }
  lit(hard doorway backlight rims the fingers; the felt fibers glow at the edge)
  aud{ soft felt rasp under the fingertips, one slow breath, room silence }
  sty{ same stock }
