=== FTL-1 KEY (how to read the program below) ===
You are reading FTL-1, the Funnel-Tree Language: a compressed program
describing one video. Decode it with these rules:
1. SEED:: is the concept in plain words.
2. LAW:: sets authority between element types:
   ID(identity) > AR(archetype) > MO(mood) > WO(world) > DE(scene).
   If two elements ever disagree, obey the higher one and adapt the lower.
3. NODE blocks each describe one element. Their six keys mean:
   form=shape/anatomy  mat=surface/texture  mot=how it moves
   lit=how light behaves on it  snd=the sound it makes  phy=physics acting on it.
4. RESOLVE:: lines (A (x) B -> C) are pre-decided conflicts: always apply C.
5. FREEZE blocks are canon. $BIBLE = the character exactly as written,
   $WORLD = the location exactly as written. Expand them in full wherever
   referenced. Never reinterpret, restyle, or abbreviate them.
6. SHOT blocks are renderable clips:
   cam()=camera+lens  act{}=action  lit()=lighting  aud{}=all audio
   anc()=continuity element carried from the previous shot  sty{}=film style.
7. Render ONLY the shot named on the RENDER:: line, as one continuous
   photorealistic cinematic clip. Everything not in that shot is context.
=== END KEY ===

=== FTL-1Q ADDENDUM (cinema-grade quality laws) ===
13. LOOK { } is a frozen craft block -- the film's photographic DNA. Its keys
    (dof, grain, palette, light-source, camera-speed) apply to EVERY shot,
    on top of that shot's sty{}.
14. QUALITY LAWS -- obey in every shot:
    - ONE small, precise physical action per shot; hands, faces, and props
      do the acting. Small exact motion renders masterfully; big motion breaks.
    - Every lit() names the physical light source inside the scene (window,
      doorway, lamp, fire, suns). Never unmotivated light.
    - Shallow depth of field by default: one sharp subject plane, creamy
      falloff. Deep focus only when a shot explicitly asks.
    - The camera moves slowly or not at all; the subject supplies the motion.
    - Every surface carries age: wear, dust, patina, scratches, skin texture.
15. Every sequence contains at least one INSERT shot -- an extreme close-up
    of hands, a prop, or a material detail on a macro lens. It is the film's
    most tactile moment.
=== END ADDENDUM ===
